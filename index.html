QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Dairy Classic’s ingredients, production capacity, storage guidelines, and retail availability.

Dairy Classic products are manufactured in our modern high-tech production facilities in Tashkent (БУТ 5, Tashkent) and Samarkand, Uzbekistan. Under Foreign Company Singapore Samarkand, we operate world-class European processing machinery under stringent international quality and hygiene standards.

Since 1996, our motto has been "Healthy Living". We craft our recipes using 100% pure fresh dairy cream and milk, Madagascar bourbon vanilla, authentic Belgian cacao, and handpicked fruits with zero artificial compromises.

Our modernized ice cream plant boasts an 80,000-litre daily production capacity, ensuring every supermarket, cafe, and retail partner across Uzbekistan receives fresh, premium-grade ice creams daily.

For optimum texture and flavor retention, store your Dairy Classic ice creams at -18°C (0°F) or colder. For 1-Litre Tubs, allowing them to rest at room temperature for 1-2 minutes before scooping yields the silkiest texture.

Dairy Classic is available at over 2,500+ retail stores and leading supermarket chains across Uzbekistan, including Korzinka, Makro, Havas, and neighborhood gourmet shops in Tashkent, Samarkand, Bukhara, and Fergana.

We welcome retail and distribution partners. You can contact our commercial sales office in Tashkent at +998 78 120 33 77 / 78 or via email at info@dairyclassics.com to discuss wholesale supply and branded freezer placement.
